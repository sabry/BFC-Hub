📝 Policy Committee Chairs

Monthly meetings (Wells House) that the BFC President-Elect organizes and chairs, under the BFC President’s invitation. A forum for school-level Policy Committee Chairs to compare policy developments across schools, coordinate faculty-led policy work, and bring common concerns to the BFC and to campus/university policy processes.

  • Priority: faculty-led models for syllabus guidance on generative AI
  • Priority: review new/revised university policies for school-level implications; coordinate responses protecting peer review, due process, academic freedom, and faculty governance
  • Priority: examine proposed program changes, reorganizations, mergers, or closures through appropriate school governance
  • Past topics: SEA 202 and post-tenure review
  • Logistics: Microsoft Teams for communication/document sharing; remote participation offered each meeting
  • Scheduling note: BTAA conference (Dec 3–5) overlaps one monthly meeting — reschedule or arrange a substitute
  • Scope note (Heather, Aug 2026): overlap with BFC/UFC committees is fine — this channel exists for faculty leaders (school policy chairs) who aren't BFC/UFC representatives. For a specific agenda topic, invite the relevant committee co-chairs that day (e.g., EPC/TPC co-chairs for an AI guidelines topic).

🏛️ State / National Issues

State legislation and federal/national developments outside IU Bloomington that affect the faculty.

  • Federal: "Duration of status" rule (replaces indefinite F-1/J-1 admission with a fixed term, ~4 years, plus 30-day grace period) — finalized by DHS July 16–17, 2026 ↗
  • SEA 202 (2024) — tenure decisions tied to "intellectual diversity" criteria; mandatory 5-year post-tenure review for tenured faculty ↗
  • HEA 1001 (2025 state budget bill) — makes faculty senate/council resolutions advisory-only; restructures the Board of Trustees (all 9 seats now gubernatorial appointments); adds tenured-faculty "productivity reviews"; bars retired/emeritus faculty from voting in governance bodies (I.C. 21-38-11-3) — this provision is under active litigation, which BFC endorsed in Sept. 2025 ↗
  • SEA 289 (2025) — removes the requirement that public university boards maintain diversity committees; revises minority-scholarship eligibility criteria (note: IU's own DEI office closures in May 2025 went further than this law strictly required) ↗
  • SEA 199 (2026) — state review of low-enrollment/low-earning-outcome degree programs; could affect some IU Bloomington programs; final list due 2027 ↗
  • SEA 76 (2026) — bars postsecondary institutions from limiting cooperation with immigration enforcement; civil penalties up to $10,000/violation ↗
  • 2026 Indiana legislative session concluded Feb 27, 2026 — next regular session is 2027; watch interim study committees (esp. SEA 199 follow-up) over the fall ↗
